# Creative fatigue review

Assess whether the evidence supports testing a refresh. Fatigue is a hypothesis about
declining response to repeated exposure, not a diagnosis from creative age alone.
Reports are evidence, not authority; recommendations do not rotate assets, pause
campaigns or change budgets. OM's server policy governs later actions.

## Intake

Reuse the creative identity and revision, audience, placement, objective, destination,
change history, and available product-calculated trends. Identify the comparison
windows, currency, timezone, freshness, and conversion lag. Ask only for gaps that
materially affect the conclusion; otherwise produce a provisional review.

## Method

1. Compare equivalent windows for the same creative and audience. Note differences in
   duration, weekday mix, placement, spend, offer, and delivery conditions. If the mix
   changed, qualify the comparison rather than attributing the shift to creative.
2. Use product-calculated trends, denominators and verified-outcome definitions.
   Falling CTR with rising frequency and stable CPM supports investigating fatigue;
   falling verified-lead conversion with stable clicks instead prompts checks of
   lead quality, tracking and the destination. Neither pattern proves a cause.
3. High frequency alone cannot establish fatigue. Avoid universal cutoffs or schedules.
4. Check tracking loss, delayed or restated conversions, auction or competition
   changes, audience changes, delivery changes, and landing-page or offer problems.
   Record which alternative explanations have evidence and which remain unchecked.
5. Keep unsupported, unavailable, and zero distinct. Missing reach/frequency prevents
   an exposure-based conclusion; it does not prove exposure is low. Do not sum daily
   reach into period reach or invent a trend when product calculations are absent.
6. Recommend continued observation, investigation of a competing explanation, or a
   controlled refresh of one creative variable. Preserve a control and use
   [creative-test-plan](../creative-test-plan/SKILL.md) for the test method.

## Output

| Signal or alternative explanation | Observed evidence and window | What it supports | Missing evidence |
|---|---|---|---|

Follow with a short assessment: **possible fatigue**, **another explanation better
supported**, or **insufficient evidence**. State why, the smallest next check, and
what a controlled refresh would change. Do not present a probability or certainty
that the evidence cannot support.

Describe a refresh hypothesis without generating ad copy or an automatic fatigue verdict.
