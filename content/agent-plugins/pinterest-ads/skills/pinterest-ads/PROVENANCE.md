## Vendor facts checked

- [Ad specifications](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/pinterest-product-specs) — fetched 2026-10-28: standard-image PNG/JPEG; recommended 2:3, 1000 x 1500 pixels; title maximum 100 characters with feed truncation; description maximum 800, used for relevance and not shown in feeds.
- [Targeting overview](https://help.pinterest.com/en/business/article/targeting-overview) — fetched 2026-10-28: ad-group keyword, interest and customer/site-visitor/engagement audience targeting; expansion can extend selected interests/keywords. No budget minimum or bid figure stated.
