## Marketing guidance

This guidance is advisory; use only tools listed in the OM catalog; any launch, budget or spend change goes through OM's server approval.

### Structure

- Connect each campaign's intended outcome to its ad-group audience and Pins.
  Keep product/offer themes recognisable from the Pin through the destination.
- Review the selected objective and optimization event in current Pinterest
  docs and account evidence; a platform option need not be available in OM.

### Targeting

- Use keywords for relevant searches, interests for related topics, and audience
  targeting for supplied customer, site-visitor or Pinterest-engagement groups.
- Explain each group's discovery or returning-audience purpose. Review selected
  criteria and any expansion in account evidence before describing its reach;
  a narrow keyword list alone does not prove narrow delivery.

### Creative

- For standard image ads, Pinterest recommends 2:3 at 1000 x 1500 pixels;
  PNG or JPEG is supported. Treat this size as a recommendation, not a minimum.
- Standard-image titles allow up to 100 characters and may be truncated in feeds.
  Descriptions allow up to 800 characters, support delivery relevance, and are
  not displayed in feeds; put the visible offer in the image/title.
- Keep the Pin's product, availability and offer consistent with its destination.
  Check current Pinterest docs for video or other formats; these image specs
  are not a universal creative contract.

### Budget/bidding

- Match the proposed optimization outcome to the event evidence actually
  available; a plan to drive purchases needs more than Pin engagement data.
- Assess budget viability for that audience and outcome. Check current Pinterest
  docs/account options for bidding; do not invent a universal daily minimum or
  assume an automatic mode is eligible or exposed through OM.

### Measurement pitfalls

- Distinguish saves and other Pin engagement from outbound visits and purchases.
  A popular Pin alone does not establish profitable site traffic.
- Review destination and tracking consistency when a Pin is reused across
  campaigns. State which campaign and window supplied the evidence; unavailable
  reporting remains unknown rather than proof of no sales.
